Thank you for installing Neuron!

To access the service within the cluster:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "neuron.fullname" . }} 8000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

If Ingress is enabled, browse:
  http{{ if .Values.ingress.tls.enabled }}s{{ end }}://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}/app/

Health checks:
  curl http://localhost:8000/health/live
  curl http://localhost:8000/health/ready

Metrics (requires key if configured):
  curl -H "X-API-Key: <PREDICT_API_KEY>" http://localhost:8000/metrics

Proxy readiness (predict/admin key may be required):
  curl -H "X-API-Key: <PREDICT_API_KEY>" http://localhost:8000/proxy/ready
